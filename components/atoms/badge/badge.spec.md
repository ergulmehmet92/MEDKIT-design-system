# mk-badge

**Purpose:** A small static label: Draft, New, v2.0.0.
**Atomic level:** Atom.
**Status:** Ready. Matches `Controls`.

## API

Every native `span` prop, plus `tone`: `"neutral"` (default), `"accent"` or `"version"`.

## Look

A small stadium in the label face, uppercase. Neutral is a hairline outline; accent is a honey tint with a honey rim, from `color.text.accent`; version keeps its case on a faint fill, after a honey dot. In forced colours every badge takes a CanvasText border.

## Rules

Badges label things. They never say whether something works, is live or has failed: that is StatusPill, which always has a lamp and a word. Keep badges to one or two words.

## Lineage

**Contains:** nothing. **Used in:** the studio's drafts, the kit's version marker, the portfolio's post labels.
