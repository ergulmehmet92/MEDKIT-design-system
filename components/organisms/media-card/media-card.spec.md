# mk-media-card

**Purpose:** A video: YouTube long-form or a Short.
**Atomic level:** Organism. **Status:** Ready. Matches `Cards`.

Props: `title`, `href`, `format` (`"long"` 16:9 or `"short"` 9:16), `duration`, `thumbnail`, `published`, `source` ("YouTube"), `summary` (long-form only), `titleAs`. The duration on the picture carries the format, so there is no format badge. A Short's meta says "Short". A missing thumbnail keeps the frame and the duration.

**Contains:** Card. **Used in:** the portfolio's videos, the YouTube studio.
