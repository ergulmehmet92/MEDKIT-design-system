# mk-footer

**Purpose:** The site's footer.
**Atomic level:** Organism. **Status:** Ready. Matches `Cards`.

Props: `name`, `thesis`, `groups` (`{ title, links }[]`), `smallPrint` (items left to right), `navLabel` ("Footer"). A `footer` landmark holding a named `nav` with a heading per group. From 48rem of its own width the name sits beside three link lists; below that it stacks, with two lists per row. Links are underlined at 30% and solid on hover, with a 4px vertical pad for a larger target.

**Contains:** nothing; a version Badge often goes in the small print. **Used in:** every page of the portfolio.
