# Data

Charts tell one story each, in few colours, with every number reachable without a mouse.

Eight categorical colours, in a fixed order, stay for views with several series of equal weight. Amber and blue scales carry magnitude; a blue to amber scale carries polarity around a neutral middle.

## Rules

- One story series wears the lead colour; comparisons wear grey.
- Green and red mean up and down, and nothing else; every value they mark carries a sign or an arrow.
- Honey marks one thing per chart: a forecast, a highlighted part.
- Keys appear on hover or focus, and pin with the Key button.
- Every chart has a crosshair or nearest-point reading, arrow-key reading with a live region, and a table.
- Bars start at zero, are 24px at most, and are rounded at the data end only.
- Tables are real tables: numbers right in tabular figures, headers that sort and say so.
