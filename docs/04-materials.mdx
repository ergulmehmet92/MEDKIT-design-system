# Materials

Everything sits over the **living light**: a slow WebGL light in the theme's ambient colours, with fine grain. It holds still for reduced motion and falls back to a gradient without WebGL. Posters use **Blaze**, the light rebuilt from sage, sand, highlight and honey, so cut-through type keeps 6.9:1.

Over the light, three materials:

| Material | Used for | How it's made |
|---|---|---|
| **Glass** | Panels and cards | The surface at 58% over a 22px blur, a specular hairline, a soft shade, 18px corners |
| **Lens** | Quiet buttons, chips, tabs, fields | Clear glass that bends the light at its rim, with a highlight facing the pointer's light; fields are a lens pressed in |
| **Eclipse** | The one primary action, pressed chips | An opaque body that blocks the light, which spills past its edge |

## Shapes

One-line controls are stadiums; text areas take 14px corners; panels and cards take `radius.panel`, 18px. Surfaces outside glass stay square.

## Fallbacks

Without backdrop-filter, or when someone asks for less transparency, glass turns solid. In forced colours, materials become CanvasText borders on Canvas.
