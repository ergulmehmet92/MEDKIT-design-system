# Type

Two faces. **TeX Gyre Heros** sets everything people read; **Share Tech Mono** sets labels: eyebrows, kinds, table headers and meta lines, always uppercase with open tracking. Chart text is the body face too.

## Poster moments

PosterHeadline uses the poster size, leading and tracking: very large, very tight, bold, cut through to the light. Use it once on a page, for a few words.

## Rules

- Numbers that compare sit in tabular figures.
- Use a real minus, −, for negative numbers, and signs on changes.
- Headlines wrap without hyphens; balance them with `text-wrap: balance`.
